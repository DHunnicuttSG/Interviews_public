Likely Mock Question
A very Karat-style question could be:
"You notice customer retention dropped from 45% to 35% over the last quarter. Describe how you would investigate, what SQL you would write, what analyses you would perform, and what conclusions you would try to reach."
That's combining both parts of the interview: SQL extraction plus analytical reasoning. In my experience, candidates who can clearly structure their investigation and explain tradeoffs often outperform candidates with stronger coding skills but weaker business thinking.
